\begin{callout}
\textbf{Purpose.} Leave with a \textbf{clear, written, agreed objective} for the project (it
sets $\sim$35--40\% of our grade), \emph{and} pressure-test the direction we've researched ---
positioning Creo for the AI value-creation / ``AI roll-up'' wave --- against what management
actually wants and can credibly deliver.

\smallskip
\textbf{Stance.} This direction is our \emph{recommendation}, not an assumption. Tier 1 tests
appetite; if it's not the fit, we adapt. \gap marks an input we need to build the plan.
\end{callout}

% ======================================================================
\section{Tier 1 --- Objectives \& appetite (must lock at this meeting)}
% ======================================================================
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \gap\textbf{What do you most want from this engagement?} What's the 2027 revenue goal,
  and what does a ``win'' look like to you? (We lead with \emph{your} objective, not ours.)
  \item \gap\textbf{How central is AI to where you want Creo to go} --- a direction you want to
  \emph{own}, an adjacency, or not a near-term priority? Our research points to a strong opening
  in AI value creation; we want to confirm that fits before we build on it.
  \item \textbf{Are you willing to be the contrarian, rigor-over-hype voice} --- telling PE
  buyers what AI \emph{can't} durably do --- in a market that's mostly hype? The positioning
  depends on it.
  \item \textbf{When you grade us in December, what would make this an A+?} Name the 2--3 things
  you most want to walk away with.
\end{enumerate}

% ======================================================================
\section{Tier 2 --- Creo's AI credibility (the make-or-break)}
% ======================================================================
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item \gap\textbf{What is Creo's actual AI expertise and track record today?} Who can credibly
  deliver --- and speak to --- AI diligence in front of a sophisticated PE buyer?
  \item \gap\textbf{Is there a genuine AI practitioner in-house, or would you acquire / partner /
  embed one?} The ``we tell you what's real about AI'' position only works if Creo is
  \emph{demonstrably} real on AI --- otherwise it's the hype we're critiquing.
  \item \gap\textbf{Are you interested in adding staff in the AI area} --- building genuine
  in-house AI capability? What's the appetite, budget, timeline, and seniority?
  \item \textbf{Build a small AI delivery team, or hire just to diligence?} Do you want to staff
  up to \emph{deliver} automation, or hire only enough to \emph{credibly assess and architect}
  it while vendors do the build?
  \item \textbf{How would you find and vet real AI hires?} Genuine AI experts are scarce and the
  market is full of people overclaiming --- worth applying Creo's own ``is this AI real?'' rigor
  to its own hiring.
  \item \textbf{Are you comfortable with an ``orchestrate, don't build'' model} --- Creo is the
  independent diligence-and-architecture brain; specialist vendors do the actual AI build?
\end{enumerate}

% ======================================================================
\section{Tier 3 --- The offer \& proof}
% ======================================================================
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item \textbf{Would you offer a free ``AI-Automatability Diagnostic''} as the lead magnet ---
  and stand behind it as Creo's signature?
  \item \gap\textbf{Is there a target or portfolio company we could run a pilot diagnostic on,}
  for a proof case? (One real result beats any deck.)
  \item \gap\textbf{What can we cite} --- named clients, real outcomes --- and what are the
  confidentiality boundaries?
\end{enumerate}

% ======================================================================
\section{Tier 4 --- Distribution \& the buyer}
% ======================================================================
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item \gap\textbf{Which sponsors in your network are doing or seriously weighing AI roll-ups?}
  Which warm relationships do we lead with? (Your distribution edge over the big firms.)
  \item \textbf{Who's the economic buyer} --- operating partner, deal partner, fund principal,
  portfolio-company CEO?
  \item \textbf{What's the biggest objection a PE buyer raises} to paying for AI diligence, and
  how do you answer it today?
\end{enumerate}

% ======================================================================
\section{Tier 5 --- The marketing engine}
% ======================================================================
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item \textbf{Would Rich lead the founder-driven LinkedIn content,} and are you up for
  reviving the survey motion as an annual ``AI Reality Report''?
  \item \textbf{Are you comfortable publishing contrarian, evidence-based takes} (e.g.\ ``most AI
  pilots fail,'' ``the AI layoffs aren't what they look like'')?
  \item \gap\textbf{Current marketing infrastructure} --- website, CRM/HubSpot, lead capture ---
  what exists, and is there a budget for execution in 2027?
\end{enumerate}

% ======================================================================
\section{Tier 6 --- Primary research access}
% ======================================================================
\begin{enumerate}[resume]
  \item \gap\textbf{Will you introduce us to 3--5 PE buyers} (sponsors / operating partners) to
  interview on their AI-roll-up intentions, where pilots stall, and willingness to pay?
  \item \textbf{Any existing client feedback, win/loss notes, or AI-pilot data} we can start from?
\end{enumerate}

% ======================================================================
\section{Logistics to confirm before we leave}
% ======================================================================
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Primary contact \& weekly cadence} --- Rich, Akshita, or both? Best day/time?
  \item \textbf{Others to talk to} internally (e.g.\ Jacqueline on marketing/research)?
  \item \textbf{Deliverable format} for the December hand-off.
  \item \textbf{Next step + owner} for each open item above.
\end{itemize}

% ======================================================================
\section{Prep notes for the team}
% ======================================================================
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Propose, don't impose.} We're recommending a direction; lead by understanding
  what management wants, test the AI appetite (Tier 1), and adapt if they want something else.
  The grade rides on hitting \emph{their} objective.
  \item \textbf{Bring the research + draft plan as background} --- surface the AI thesis as a
  recommendation, with the honest caveats (rigor over hype; the real handoff ratio is a map, not
  ``1000:1''). Don't oversell.
  \item Assign a \textbf{lead asker per tier} and a \textbf{note-taker}; capture the agreed
  objective verbatim --- that wording is what we're graded against.
\end{itemize}
